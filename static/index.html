Why does demand affect warehouse placement? (Mathematical Principle)

GRIDPOINT minimizes Total Geographic Delivery Distance weighted by demand ($\sum \text{demand}_i \times \text{distance}_{ij}$). High-demand delivery zones exert far greater mathematical pull on warehouse location than low-demand zones:

High-Demand Zone (e.g. Koramangala)
420 orders/day × 2.4 km = 1,008 order-km
Reducing distance here yields massive delivery cost and fleet savings.
Low-Demand Zone (e.g. Kengeri)
150 orders/day × 14.0 km = 2,100 order-km
Lower order volume reduces its influence on optimal warehouse placement.
